Privacy Policy

This app is a personal content-publishing tool used solely by its creator to publish videos and images from the "Barrão e Mijado" animated series to the creator's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

OAuth access tokens are encrypted at rest and used only to publish content the creator explicitly authorizes. No data is shared with, or sold to, any third party. The app is not available to other users and does not collect any data from the public.
